Sources Patañjali, Yoga Sūtra 2.38, on brahmacharya.
Jung, “On Psychic Energy” (1928), Collected Works vol. 8, and Symbols of Transformation, CW 5, on libido as psychic energy.
The Secret of the Golden Flower, translated by Richard Wilhelm with a commentary by Jung (1929); Jung’s commentary is in CW 13. Its central method is “turning the light around”; the film paraphrases it.
Taoist inner alchemy (neidan): refining jing (essence) into qi (breath, vital energy) into shen (spirit).
